About Mee

Hi, I’m Nathan!

I’m a multidisciplinary designer who enjoys working across branding, product, and digital experiences.

I didn’t start with design – I started with curiosity. I’ve always been interested in how things work, and over time that turned into building things that solve real problems. Growing up around a social enterprise shaped how I think about design – not just as something that looks good, but something that should be useful and meaningful.

Today, I work across different areas – from brand identity and packaging to UI and digital products – depending on what the problem needs. I like being involved end-to-end, from idea to execution.
